Planetary overview

A small world with a remarkable story.

Earth systems

Four connected spheres.

Earth's major systems continuously exchange matter and energy, creating the conditions in which life can flourish.

🌊

Hydrosphere

All of Earth's water — oceans, rivers, lakes, groundwater, glaciers, and water vapor — moves through a global water cycle.

☁️

Atmosphere

A thin layer of gases surrounds the planet, regulating climate, filtering radiation, and providing the air that living organisms need.

⛰️

Geosphere

The solid Earth includes the crust, mantle, and core. Plate tectonics continually reshapes continents and ocean basins.

🌱

Biosphere

Life occupies environments from deep oceans to high mountains, interacting with the atmosphere, oceans, rocks, and climate.

☀️

Energy

Sunlight drives weather, photosynthesis, and much of Earth's surface activity, while internal heat powers geological processes.

🌙

The Moon

Earth's natural satellite helps stabilize the planet's axial tilt and produces tides through its gravitational influence.

Inside Earth

A layered planet.

Beneath the surface, enormous pressure and heat create a structure that can be understood through four major layers.

Crust

The thin, rigid outer shell on which continents and ocean floors are found.

Mantle

A vast layer of hot rock whose slow movement helps drive plate tectonics.

Outer Core

A liquid layer dominated by iron and nickel; its motion generates Earth's magnetic field.

Inner Core

A solid, extremely hot central region kept solid by immense pressure.

Our shared home

The future of Earth is also a human question.

Climate change, biodiversity loss, pollution, and resource use influence the resilience of natural systems. Understanding Earth is the first step toward making informed decisions about its future.

Protect biodiversity

Healthy ecosystems provide food, clean water, pollination, carbon storage, and countless other benefits.

Understand climate

Long-term observations help us distinguish natural variability from human-driven changes in the climate system.

Think globally

Air, oceans, ecosystems, and climate cross political borders. Many planetary challenges therefore require cooperation.
